## 7. Readiness Decision

### READY WITH ASSUMPTIONS

**Meaning:** 5-concept **exploration** may begin only if every concept uses labeled placeholders and the assumptions below. Do **not** treat exploration output as approved brand, messaging, or final IA. Do **not** proceed to production design, Design DNA, or build until Section 2 items are answered.

### Explicit assumptions (provisional — replace with client answers)

1. **Business frame (placeholder):** Alool Technologies is presented as a technology practice that delivers websites and digital products using a research-led, AI-assisted workflow, with ADI as internal design intelligence — **not** confirmed as the final public definition.
2. **Offerings (structural only):** Concepts include a Services/Capabilities region with unlabeled or generic capability slots until the real catalog arrives; no invented service names presented as fact.
3. **ADI visibility (variable):** Concepts may explore different ADI prominence levels; none is approved until the client chooses.
4. **Audience (placeholder):** Concepts address a cautious first-time evaluator of Alool’s work — not a confirmed segment list.
5. **Primary goal (placeholder):** Establish a clear, credible public presence that leads to contact — not a confirmed KPI set.
6. **Primary action (placeholder):** A single primary “Contact / Inquire” path; channel specifics TBD.
7. **Differentiation (placeholder):** Research-before-build and human-curated AI-assisted delivery may appear as provisional themes, clearly marked as unapproved marketing copy.
8. **Brand (provisional system):** Concepts invent **temporary** type/color systems within practice preferences for exploration only; real brand may replace them entirely.
9. **Proof (flexible):** Prefer process/method proof patterns; portfolio-led concepts remain optional until publishable work is confirmed.
10. **Language / market (default):** Single-language exploration unless intake requires multilingual architecture.
11. **Competitors:** None named on concepts until the client provides a market competitor list.

### If assumptions are rejected
Outcome becomes **NOT READY** until Section 4 questions are answered.
