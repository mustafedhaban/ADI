# Alool Technologies Website Redesign
## Client Discovery Questionnaire

**Project:** Alool Technologies Website Redesign  
**Purpose:** Collect client-owned information needed before website design begins.

Please answer as completely as possible. Short answers are fine. If something is unknown or not decided yet, write **TBD** and note who will decide.

---

## 1. Company & Business

1. **In one or two sentences, what does Alool Technologies do?**  
   *Why it matters:* This becomes the core of the homepage and company introduction.

2. **What is Alool Technologies’ legal / trading name for public use?** (e.g. exact spelling for the website header and footer)

3. **When was the company founded?** (year is enough)

4. **Roughly how large is the company today?** (e.g. solo practice, small team, larger organization — no need for confidential detail)

5. **Is there a current public website URL we should review?**  
   *Note:* During research setup, `alool.tech` was unreachable and `alool.com` appeared to be a contact placeholder only. Please confirm the correct URL, or confirm that no usable site exists yet.

6. **If there is no usable current site, what has Alool used so far for public presence?** (e.g. LinkedIn, proposals only, partner listings)

---

## 2. Services & Solutions

7. **List every service or solution Alool wants shown on the website.**  
   For each item, include a short plain-language description (1–2 sentences).

8. **Which of those offerings are primary (must be prominent) vs secondary (can be shorter or linked)?**

9. **Are any offerings products (sold/licensed), services (delivered as projects), or both?**  
   *Why it matters:* Product and service pages are structured differently.

10. **Should any internal tools or methods be named publicly?** If yes, which ones, and at what level of detail?  
    *Why it matters:* Avoids over- or under-explaining proprietary methods on a company site.

11. **What should not be listed as an offering?** (out of scope, discontinued, or not yet public)

---

## 3. Target Audiences

12. **Who are Alool’s ideal clients?** Describe them in plain language (organization type, size, sector, or role — whatever is accurate).

13. **Who typically makes the buying decision?** (e.g. founder, marketing lead, IT lead, board, procurement)

14. **Who influences the decision but does not buy?** (e.g. design stakeholders, technical reviewers)

15. **Are there audiences the site should welcome but not prioritize?**

16. **Are there audiences the site should not target?**

---

## 4. Positioning & Differentiation

17. **What makes Alool different from alternatives a client might consider?**  
    Please give concrete differences, not slogans.

18. **What do current clients typically say they value most about working with Alool?**

19. **What misconceptions should the website correct?** (e.g. what people wrongly assume Alool is or does)

20. **In three words or a short phrase, how should visitors describe Alool after visiting the site?**  
    *Why it matters:* Guides tone and visual direction without inventing brand claims.

---

## 5. Website Goals

21. **What is the primary business goal of this website?** Choose one primary goal, then list secondary goals if needed.  
    Examples only (pick what fits, or write your own): generate qualified inquiries · explain what Alool does · support sales conversations · recruit · publish thought leadership · other.

22. **How will you know the website is successful 3–6 months after launch?** (metrics or qualitative signals)

23. **Is there a hard launch deadline or event the site must support?**

---

## 6. Content & Messaging

24. **What existing written content can be reused?** (about text, service descriptions, bios, blog posts, proposals, decks)

25. **What new content must be written for launch?**

26. **Who will approve final website copy?**

27. **Are there words or claims that must appear?** (approved phrases, legal names, certifications)

28. **Are there words or claims that must never appear?**

29. **What proof points are approved for public use?** (years of experience, number of projects, notable outcomes — only if accurate and allowed)

---

## 7. Portfolio / Case Studies

30. **Which past projects can be shown publicly?** List project names or anonymized titles.

31. **For each showable project, what can we include?** (client name, logo, screenshots, live URL, results, testimonial)

32. **Which projects must remain confidential or anonymized?**

33. **If no public case studies exist yet, what substitute proof is acceptable?** (process explanation, sample work, partner endorsements, etc.)

---

## 8. Brand Identity

34. **Does Alool already have a brand identity to follow, or should visual identity be developed as part of this project?**

35. **If a brand already exists, what assets and rules are available?** (logo files, fonts, colors, voice guidelines, do’s/don’ts)

36. **If identity must be developed, what direction is preferred?** Describe freely (e.g. restrained, premium, practical, editorial). Avoid vague terms like “modern” alone — add concrete preferences.

37. **Are there visual styles Alool explicitly wants to avoid?**

38. **Is there an existing brand owner / designer we should coordinate with?**

---

## 9. Geographic Market & Localization

39. **Where does Alool operate or sell?** (countries, regions, or “global remote”)

40. **Does the website need more than one language at launch?** If yes, which languages, and which is primary?

41. **Are there regional legal, cultural, or compliance requirements the site must respect?**

---

## 10. Competitors

42. **List the main organizations clients compare Alool against.**  
    *Why it matters:* Competitive context shapes positioning. Design inspiration is not the same as market competitors.

43. **For each competitor, what do they do better — and what does Alool do better?**

44. **Are there companies Alool should not be visually or verbally associated with?**

---

## 11. Conversion & Contact

45. **What is the single most important action a visitor should take?**  
    Examples only: send an inquiry · book a call · email · call · download something · other.

46. **What contact channels should appear on the site?** (form fields, email, phone, calendar link, social profiles)

47. **Who receives inquiries, and what is the expected response time?**

48. **Should the site support multiple contact paths** (e.g. new business vs support vs careers)?

---

## 12. Technical Requirements

49. **Is there a preferred domain for the new site?** Confirm exact spelling.

50. **Are there hosting, security, or performance requirements we must follow?**

51. **Must the site integrate with any existing tools?** (analytics, CRM, email, newsletter, chat)

52. **Are there accessibility or compliance standards required?** (e.g. specific WCAG level, government/NGO requirements)

53. **Who will own domain DNS and hosting accounts?**

---

## 13. Content Management

54. **Who will update website content after launch?** (name/role)

55. **How often do you expect content to change?** (rarely / occasionally / frequently)

56. **Do editors need a visual content management system, or is a simpler handoff acceptable for v1?**  
    *Why it matters:* Affects architecture and launch scope.

57. **Which pages must be editable without a developer?**

---

## 14. Assets & Access

58. **Please provide or list available assets:**  
    - Logo files (preferably vector)  
    - Brand guidelines (if any)  
    - Fonts Alool is licensed to use  
    - Photography / video  
    - Team photos or bios  
    - Existing PDFs, decks, or one-pagers  
    - Client logos approved for use

59. **Who can grant access to domain, hosting, analytics, and any existing site accounts?**

60. **Preferred point of contact for questions during the project** (name, role, email)

---

## Required Before Design

Complete this checklist before website design begins. Items marked **must** are blockers.

- [ ] **Must:** Plain-language statement of what Alool Technologies does
- [ ] **Must:** Confirmed list of services/solutions to show on the site
- [ ] **Must:** Description of ideal clients / target audiences
- [ ] **Must:** Geographic market (where Alool operates or sells)
- [ ] **Must:** Primary business goal of the website
- [ ] **Must:** Primary visitor action (what success looks like on-site)
- [ ] **Must:** Clear differentiators (what makes Alool different)
- [ ] **Must:** Confirmed competitor list (market competitors, not design inspiration)
- [ ] **Must:** Decision on brand identity — existing brand to follow **or** identity to be developed
- [ ] **Must:** Available brand assets inventory (or confirmation that none exist yet)
- [ ] **Must:** Inventory of reusable content and publishable portfolio / case studies (or acceptable substitute proof)
- [ ] **Must:** Language requirements (single language vs multiple languages at launch)
- [ ] **Must:** Named owner for post-launch content updates
- [ ] Preferred: Domain confirmation and technical/integration constraints
- [ ] Preferred: Copy approver and inquiry routing details
